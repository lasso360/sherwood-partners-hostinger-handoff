Chheko Taing joined Sherwood Partners in 2013 and has over ten years of accounting and finance experience. He has experience in various industries including technology, retail, construction, and food & business services. Before joining Sherwood Partners, Mr. Taing managed the accounting team for various companies.

Mr. Taing is in charge of the day-to-day back-end operations of Sherwood Partners. Part of his role includes setting up and maintaining special purpose entities to act as assignee for an Assignment for the Benefit of Creditors. He is also responsible for overseeing the books for over one hundred active clients.

Mr. Taing has a degree in accounting.
