William Bremauntz has been a member of the Sherwood professional team since March 2019. Since then, he has participated in a variety of assignments, including managed wind downs, assignments for the benefit of creditors, and in asset and intellectual property sale processes.

Prior to joining Sherwood, Mr. Bremauntz was in the financial services industry. He has over 10 years of experience working for some of the top financial institutions in the nation. He spent a majority of his career in commercial and consumer banking.
