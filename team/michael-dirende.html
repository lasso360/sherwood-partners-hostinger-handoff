Michael DiRende

Practice Leader & COO

Michael DiRende brings over two and a half decades of expertise to the world of restructuring and finance, establishing himself as a seasoned professional known for assuming pivotal executive roles throughout his career. In his recent roles, Michael served as the Chief Restructuring Officer for a leading Insurtech/Insurance carrier and as the Chief Surveillance Officer for a publicly traded insurance company, where he effectively managed a substantial portfolio valued at $50 billion, specializing in structured finance insurance risks. His remarkable accomplishments extend to his role as the lead strategist and negotiator during the challenging period of the great crisis, spearheading recovery efforts that resulted in the restoration of over $4.0 billion in value.

Michael is widely recognized as an authority in special situations and mergers and acquisitions. He has demonstrated his expertise through a series of interim financial and operational executive roles, with a track record of successfully restructuring debts exceeding $75 billion.

Throughout his career, Michael has honed his expertise across diverse sectors, including Insurance, Brokerage, Manufacturing, Structured Finance, Municipal Finance, Automotive, and intricate deal structuring. He has had the privilege of collaborating with prestigious organizations, encompassing privately held consultancies like Zolfo Cooper and Arthur Anderson, as well as publicly traded insurance companies such as Assured Guaranty and MBIA.

A graduate of Pace University with a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Public Accountancy, Michael holds certifications as a Certified Public Accountant and a Certified Insolvency Restructuring Advisor. These credentials underscore his unwavering commitment to professional excellence and his deep knowledge of the field. Michael's journey is defined by his ability to navigate complex financial landscapes and deliver outstanding results, making him a trusted expert in the world of restructuring and finance.
